About

Clinical practice informed by research, teaching and public health

I am an addiction psychiatrist and academic at the Centre for Addiction Medicine, National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences (NIMHANS), Bengaluru. My work brings together clinical care, research, training and service development.

My principal interests include alcohol and tobacco use disorders, neuromodulation, digital mental health, implementation science, community models of care and evidence synthesis. I teach and mentor postgraduate trainees and collaborate with professionals and institutions in India and internationally.

Before joining NIMHANS as faculty, I served at AIIMS Deoghar, where I helped establish psychiatry, addiction-treatment, telepsychiatry and teaching services.
